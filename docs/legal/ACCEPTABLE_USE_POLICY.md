# NUVORA — Acceptable Use Policy (G5.2)

> **Status: DRAFT for legal review.**

1. **Learner safety first.** Any conduct that endangers a minor — grooming,
   harassment, inappropriate content or contact — is a policy breach with
   immediate account suspension and possible reporting to authorities.

2. **No platform abuse.** No scraping, bots, credential stuffing, reverse
   engineering, or attempts to bypass payment, access or rate-limit
   controls (cross-account ID tampering is monitored and prosecuted as
   unauthorized access).

3. **Honest identity.** One account per person; no impersonation of
   parents, tutors, institutions or NUVORA staff.

4. **No contact harvesting.** Do not collect or reuse other users' contact
   details outside the platform's intended flows.

5. **Content rules.** Lesson materials and messages must be lawful,
   curriculum-appropriate and non-defamatory. No exam fraud, no
   misrepresentation of results or credentials.

6. **Payments integrity.** Only platform payment flows; no side payments,
   no chargeback abuse, no laundering attempts (transactions are monitored).

7. **Enforcement.** Breaches lead to warnings, suspension or termination,
   retention of evidence, and cooperation with authorities. Appeals go to
   support → founder review.
