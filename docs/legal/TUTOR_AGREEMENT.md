# YK-Virtual — Tutor Agreement (G5.2)

## 1. Relationship

You are an independent contractor, not an employee. You control your
availability, subjects and (within curriculum rules) teaching approach.
YK-Virtual provides vetting, discovery, booking, escrow payment and delivery
infrastructure in exchange for a platform commission (current: ____%).

## 2. Vetting and identity

- You provide true identity, qualification and competency evidence; you
  consent to verification (documents reviewed by the vetting team, never
  shown publicly — private bucket, signed URLs).
- You update your profile when material facts change (location, subjects,
  criminal record, professional standing).
- Failure to maintain a true profile → delisting and forfeiture of held
  payouts where law permits.

## 3. Conduct (see Safeguarding Policy)

The Safeguarding Policy is incorporated by reference and is a **material
term**. Breach = immediate suspension, retained evidence, and reporting
where required. No off-platform contact with learners; no gifts; no
financial arrangements with families outside the platform.

## 4. Lessons

- Attend scheduled lessons; notify cancellations ≥ 24h in advance through
  the platform (attendance + exception flows).
- Mark attendance and write lesson notes for every delivered lesson
  (teaching console) — payouts require completed records.
- Keep live classes within the agreed curriculum; recordings only with
  recorded consent (decision #4).

## 5. Payment

- Fees are collected via the gateway and held in escrow; released on the
  weekly payout run (`process_weekly_tutor_payouts`) after delivery is
  confirmed.
- Refunds against your lessons are deducted from your wallet before the
  next release; you may dispute within 7 days (support → finance).
- You are responsible for your own tax obligations on income earned.

## 6. Intellectual property

Materials you create for lessons remain yours; you grant YK-Virtual a
non-exclusive licence to distribute them within the cohorts you teach.
Learner work and data remain the family's.

## 7. Termination

Either party may end the relationship with 14 days' notice. Immediate
termination applies for safeguarding breaches, fraud, or persistent
no-shows. On termination, held funds for delivered lessons are paid out in
the next run; pending bookings are refunded per the refund policy.
